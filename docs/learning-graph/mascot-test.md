# Finley Mascot Style Guide

This page shows all seven Finley admonition styles. Use it to verify that
images load, colors match the book theme, and text wraps cleanly around the
mascot at both desktop and mobile widths.

---

## Neutral

!!! mascot-neutral "A Note from Finley"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/neutral.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Finley the Darwin's Finch in a neutral pose">
    Use this for general sidebars, introductions, or any content
    that doesn't call for a specific emotional tone. Finley is just
    here to keep you company.

---

## Welcome

!!! mascot-welcome "Welcome, Explorer!"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/welcome.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Finley waving welcome">
    Time to evolve your thinking! In this chapter we'll discover
    how natural selection shapes life over generations. By the end,
    you'll see the living world in a completely new way.

---

## Thinking

!!! mascot-thinking "Key Insight"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/thinking.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Finley in a thinking pose">
    Notice that natural selection can only act on traits that
    already vary in the population — it cannot create new variation
    from scratch. Mutation and reproduction do that part first.

---

## Tip

!!! mascot-tip "Finley's Tip"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/tip.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Finley pointing upward with a tip">
    When reading a phylogenetic tree, always look for the most
    recent common ancestor of two species — it's the node where
    their branches last met. That tells you how closely related
    they are.

---

## Warning

!!! mascot-warning "Common Mistake"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/warning.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Finley holding up wings in a warning pose">
    Evolution does not have a goal or direction. Saying organisms
    evolve "in order to" survive is a classic misconception —
    selection acts on existing variation; it doesn't plan ahead.

---

## Encouraging

!!! mascot-encourage "You Can Do This!"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/encouraging.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Finley giving an encouraging thumbs up">
    Hardy-Weinberg can feel abstract at first — that's completely
    normal. Take it one variable at a time, and remember: you're
    adapting brilliantly!

---

## Celebration

!!! mascot-celebration "Chapter Complete!"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/celebration.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Finley celebrating with wings spread wide">
    You've just traced the full arc from variation to speciation.
    That's a major milestone on your journey through the tree of
    life. Time to evolve your thinking — on to the next chapter!
